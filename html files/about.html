About Student Hub Portal

Student Hub Portal is a web-based academic management system developed to help students access all important college services from one place. It provides a simple, organized and user-friendly interface for managing academic information.


Objectives

  1. ~ Provide easy access to student information.
  2. ~ Manage attendance records.
  3. ~ View course details and timetable.
  4. ~ Access assignments and results.
  5. ~ Receive important college notifications.
